Tournament marks (`world-cup.svg`, `olympics.svg`) are the official emblems, cached from
Wikimedia Commons vectors of the trademark holders' artwork:

- FIFA World Cup Trophy: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FIFA_World_Cup_Icon_(Campionato_mondiale_di_calcio).svg
- Olympic rings: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Olympic_rings_without_rims.svg

Both were saved with the artwork unchanged; only a `viewBox` (absent upstream, which would
break scaling in the 38px badge) plus `role`/`aria-label` were added.

The logos remain trademarks of their respective teams, leagues, and governing bodies — the
Olympic rings are additionally protected under the Olympic Charter. They are used here only
to identify followed teams and events in a personal dashboard, never as branding.
